Lumora Solutions

Privacy

Lumora Solutions respects the information submitted through this website. The public waitlist form asks for a name, business name, phone number and email address so that an enquiry can be reviewed and followed up.

How information is used

Information submitted through the waitlist is used for the purpose of reviewing the enquiry, understanding the business need described by the person submitting it, and contacting the person about Lumora's services. Information should not be submitted if it is not necessary for the enquiry.

Website operation

The website may use normal hosting, security and analytics services required to operate the site. Technical information may be processed by those services as part of delivering and protecting the website. Lumora does not ask visitors to submit passwords or sensitive personal information through the public enquiry form.

Requests about your information

If you have submitted information through the waitlist and need to ask about it, use the public contact route and identify the submission you are referring to. Do not include passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information in a website enquiry.

This page provides general website privacy information and may be updated as Lumora's systems and processes change.
