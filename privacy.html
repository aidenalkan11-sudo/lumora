Your information

Privacy, clearly explained.

This page explains what information may be submitted through the Lumora Solutions website and how it is used to respond to enquiries and operate the site.

On this page

Information collected How it is used Website operation Service providers Your requests Policy updates

Information collected

Lumora Solutions respects the information submitted through this website. The public waitlist form asks for a name, business name, phone number and email address so that an enquiry can be reviewed and followed up.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry.

How information is used

Information submitted through the waitlist is used to review the enquiry, understand the business need described, and contact the person about Lumora's services. It may also be used to manage follow-up related to that enquiry.

Website operation and analytics

The website may use standard hosting, security and analytics services needed to operate, measure and protect the site. These services may process technical information such as browser or request data as part of delivering their functions.

Service providers

Website infrastructure and enquiry handling may rely on third-party service providers. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services. The details of providers and settings may change as the website evolves.

Questions or requests about your information

If you have submitted information through the waitlist and need to ask about it, use the contact page to identify the submission you are referring to. Do not include passwords, identity documents or other sensitive information in a website enquiry.

Lumora does not ask visitors to submit passwords or sensitive personal information through the public enquiry form.

Policy updates

This page provides general website privacy information and may be updated as Lumora's systems and processes change. Please check this page periodically for the current version.
