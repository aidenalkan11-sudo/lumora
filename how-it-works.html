From your first call to a tested AI receptionist.

A clear process to understand your calls, configure your receptionist, test real scenarios and prepare it to support your team.

01

Discover

We learn what your business does, what callers usually ask, when calls are missed, and what information your team needs from each enquiry.

Business audit Review how calls are answered today and where enquiries get missed.
Opportunity mapping Identify frequent caller questions and routine call-handling tasks.
Prioritisation Prioritise the call types and enquiries your receptionist should handle first.
02

Design

We map out how the receptionist should greet callers, answer approved FAQs, collect details, respond to booking requests and hand off calls that need a person.

Workflow mapping Define greetings, questions, call paths and human hand-offs.
Tool selection Check which phone, calendar and business tools need to connect.
System architecture Design a consistent call flow that fits how your team works.
03

Build & test

We configure the receptionist with your business information and call flows, then test common questions, unclear requests, booking scenarios and calls that need your team.

Implementation Configure the receptionist and agreed integrations.
Testing Test sample calls, answers, caller details and hand-offs.
Refinement Adjust responses and call paths based on test results.
04

Launch & optimise

Once the receptionist passes testing, it can be introduced into your call workflow. We review how it handles enquiries and refine its information and call paths as your business changes.

Launch Set up the agreed go-live call handling.
Monitor Review call outcomes and any issues reported by your team.
Improve Update FAQs, call flows and handling rules when needed.

What your AI receptionist can handle.

Your receptionist is configured around your business. Features depend on your requirements, phone setup and any connected tools.

01

Answer calls and capture enquiries

Answer incoming calls, collect caller names and contact details, understand why they're calling, and pass the enquiry to the right person or workflow.

02

Frequently asked questions

Give callers consistent answers to common questions using the business information you provide, and flag requests that need a human response.

03

Appointment requests

Help callers request or arrange appointments when connected booking tools and your chosen workflow support it, and capture details for your team.

04

Call routing and hand-offs

Route or flag calls that are complex, sensitive or outside the receptionist's scope, so your team can take over where appropriate.

05

Follow-up and connected workflows

Where appropriate, connect call outcomes to follow-up messages, enquiry records or other business tools. We can also build other custom AI systems and automate wider business processes.
