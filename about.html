About Lumora Solutions

Lumora Solutions is a Melbourne, Victoria business focused primarily on AI receptionists for businesses that want to answer more calls, capture enquiries and make it easier for customers to get through. Each receptionist is tailored to the business, its services and the way its team works.

AI receptionists are our core service, but they are not the limit of what we do. We can also build other custom AI systems, connect business tools and automate processes across a business wherever it makes practical sense. We focus on the receptionist first, then identify additional opportunities based on your needs.

How Lumora works

Projects typically begin with discovery and workflow mapping, followed by system design, implementation, testing and launch. The aim is to keep automation understandable and useful for the people who operate the business, while leaving important human decisions with the team.
