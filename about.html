About Lumora Solutions

Lumora Solutions is a Melbourne, Victoria business focused on practical AI automation for modern businesses. The goal is straightforward: identify repetitive work, design a useful system around the real business process, and make the resulting workflow reliable enough for day-to-day use.

Lumora works across lead capture, lead follow-up, booking and scheduling, customer communications, workflow automation and integrations. Rather than assuming every business needs the same software, the approach is to understand the existing process first and then automate the parts that create unnecessary manual work or missed opportunities.

How Lumora works

Projects typically begin with discovery and workflow mapping, followed by system design, implementation, testing and launch. The aim is to keep automation understandable and useful for the people who operate the business, while leaving important human decisions with the team.
