Understanding the example

What those 30-day figures represent.

The four headline figures describe different parts of the same hypothetical month. They are not four separate customer case studies, and they should not be read as guaranteed outcomes.

For example, the 120 potential enquiries are a subset of the conversations that may need human follow-up. The 95 after-hours enquiries are included within the overall enquiry figure, not added on top of it.

450 calls answeredAll incoming calls handled in the illustrative month.

95 after-hours enquiriesA portion of those potential enquiries received outside normal business hours.
