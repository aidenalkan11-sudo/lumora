Understanding the example

What those 30-day figures represent.

The four headline figures describe different parts of the same 30-day customer case study.

The 120 potential job enquiries are a subset of the 450 calls answered. The 95 after-hours enquiries are included within the overall enquiry figure, not added on top of it.

450 calls answeredAll incoming calls handled in the illustrative month.
120 potential job enquiriesCalls where a caller shares a potential work requirement.
95 after-hours enquiriesA portion of those potential enquiries received outside normal business hours.
85 hours of potential admin timeAn illustrative estimate of time freed from routine call handling and message taking.
