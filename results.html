A busy home-services business

Imagine a trades business that misses calls while staff are on jobs. A configured AI receptionist could answer those calls, collect the caller’s name, contact details and job summary, then send the enquiry to the team for follow-up.

Potential operational benefit: fewer unanswered enquiries and clearer information for callbacks. Actual outcomes depend on call volume, configuration and follow-up.
