FAQ

Questions, answered.

What to expect from an AI receptionist and how a setup is tailored to your business.

Will callers know they are speaking with AI?

The greeting and call flow are configured for your business. We recommend clearly identifying the assistant as AI rather than trying to pass it off as a human.

How long does setup take?

For a straightforward business, configuration and testing can often be completed within an hour of the consultation. More complex call flows or integrations may take longer.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Often, yes. The available options depend on your phone provider and current setup. We check compatibility before recommending any changes.

Can it connect to my existing software?

Potentially. Integrations depend on the software, available API or connection options, permissions and the workflow required. We confirm feasibility before including an integration in a proposal.

What if the AI cannot answer a question?

It should not guess. It can explain that the team will need to confirm, capture the caller’s details and flag the enquiry for follow-up. A live transfer can be configured where the phone system supports it.

Can callers speak to a person?

The call flow can offer a callback or transfer to a team member where supported by the phone setup.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on expected call volume, the features and call flows required, and any integrations. We’ll explain the setup and ongoing costs before you decide whether to proceed.

Can it answer after hours?

Yes, it can be configured to answer after-hours calls or calls your team cannot get to, depending on your phone routing setup.
