How it works

We don't automate for the sake of it.

Every automation starts with a business problem. We find where your team is losing time or opportunities, design a system around it, and put it into practice without adding unnecessary complexity.

The Lumora process.

A straightforward process designed to make automation useful, measurable and easy for your team to work with.

01

Discover

We start by understanding how your business currently handles enquiries, customers, bookings and repetitive administration. The goal is to find the highest-value opportunities for automation.

Business audit Review your current workflow and identify bottlenecks.
Opportunity mapping Find repetitive tasks that can be improved.
Prioritisation Focus on changes with the clearest potential impact.
02

Design

Once we know what needs improving, we map out exactly how the new workflow should operate. Every step has a purpose, from the initial enquiry through to the final hand-off.

Workflow mapping Plan every trigger, action and hand-off.
Tool selection Choose practical tools that fit your existing setup.
System architecture Build a reliable process instead of isolated automations.
03

Build & test

We build the automation and test it against real-world scenarios. This includes checking that information moves correctly, messages trigger at the right time and exceptions are handled properly.

Implementation Connect the required tools and workflows.
Testing Run multiple scenarios before anything goes live.
Refinement Fix friction points and improve the workflow.
04

Launch & optimise

After testing, the system goes live. We monitor how the workflow performs and make adjustments where necessary so it continues working as your business changes.

Launch Put the finished workflow into production.
Monitor Keep an eye on performance and potential issues.
Improve Refine the system as new opportunities appear.
